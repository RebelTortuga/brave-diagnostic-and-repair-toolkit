## Limitations

Backups exclude complete session/open-tab data, history, cookies, website storage, autofill and downloads. Password portability and Brave Wallet/Rewards recovery are not guaranteed. Full-profile backup and independent restore-category selection are not implemented. Earlier BraveBackupEssentials1 backups must be kept with their earlier utility; this release restores BraveToolkit2 backups only.
